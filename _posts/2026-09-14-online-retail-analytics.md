84% of revenue came from a single market, and 34% of customers never returned after a first purchase. Underneath a healthy top line, there was almost no cushion.

I built a PostgreSQL cleaning pipeline, scored 4,338 customers via RFM segmentation, and found that just 434 VIP customers generate 61% of total revenue, confirming retention, not acquisition, is what actually sustains the business.
